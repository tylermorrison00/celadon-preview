Questions

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat.

Ye website kya hai?

Creators Automation TikTok aur YouTube creators ke liye AI prompts ki library hai. Yahan tayyar prompts milte hain jinhe copy karke apni videos ke liye istemal kar sakte ho.

Kya me free me prompts use kar sakta hun?

Haan, free prompts sab ke liye hain. Account banao, pasandida prompts ko star karo aur copy karo. Kuch premium prompts Pro members ke liye hain.

Pro plan me kya extra milta hai?

Pro me poori prompt library, naye prompts 2 hafte pehle, niches 2 hafte pehle aur priority support milta hai. Basic me poori library aur weekly naye prompts hain.

Payment kese karun?

WhatsApp par rabta karo. Easypaisa se payment karo aur apna email aur transaction ID bhejo. Account upgrade kar diya jayega.

Kya me apne liye custom prompt banwa sakta hun?

Haan, Custom Prompts ka option hai. Apni niche batao, 24 ghante me tayyar prompt mil jayega. Saath me 2 free updates bhi hain.

Account kese banau?

Upar 'Start free' par click karo, email se signup karo. Koi card nahi chahiye. Free account foran ban jayega.
